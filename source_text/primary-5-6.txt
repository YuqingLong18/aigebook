5.6 Moving Toward the Future
Learning Objectives
(1)	Understand future trends in artificial intelligence.
(2)	Learn about [RED]Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)[/RED] and its powerful capabilities.
(3)	Reflect on the ultimate goals of AI and how we should live alongside it.

Since 1956, artificial intelligence has been developing for nearly 70 years, going through multiple peaks and downturns. Every time people believed AI was about to be created, various challenges would arise, and development would be pushed back into a low period. Today, we are once again standing at a historic crossroads. A new AI revolution has swept across the world like a storm. So, where will AI go in the future? Further,  how should we prepare ourselves as the era of intelligence arrives?

1. No Predicted End Point
So far, AI’s abilities have been widely recognized. Simply opening any large language model app allows you to experience its impressive ability to write reports, polish essays, summarize meeting notes, suggest travel plans, and more. Artists are using AI to create, musicians to compose, and poets to spark inspiration. These are no longer imagined scenarios.These things are happening around us every day.

If the scholars who attended the Dartmouth Conference in 1956 could see today’s achievements, they would be astonished. AI now possesses capabilities far beyond what they imagined, and in some tasks, it even matches or is able to surpass human ability. AI is galloping down a road full of promise, but no one can predict what lies at the end.

2. Intelligence Beyond Humans
In recent years, especially after the rise of large models, many people have realized that AI will eventually surpass humans in many abilities.

In the past, machines were cold, rigid, and only able to follow instructions. Later, they learned from data, though limited by model design and training scope. Different tasks, like translation and search, for example, required entirely different models. This kind of intelligence was “narrow intelligence,” or confined to one domain. The emergence of large language models changed the need to have specialized models  dramatically. By learning from massive datasets, machines gained a broad, integrative knowledge. Now, with just a task description, AI can find solutions which show increasingly powerful “general intelligence.”

With more data and better algorithms, AI’s  knowledge and ability will continue to grow. It is foreseeable that AI will surpass ordinary people in many tasks. In September 2024, when OpenAI released OpenAI o1, which significantly improved reasoning abilities, it performed better not only than most people, but also better even than the majority of highly educated individuals in specific tasks. One day, AI may even surpass the very best human experts in almost all areas.

Beyond this, AI can also use tools to act in the physical world. For example, AI can control a drone inspecting a wind farm. It can observe video feeds, analyze what actions to take, generate commands, convert them into code, and send them back to the drone. This makes drone control more flexible and stable, while the AI “brain” gains experience from real-world interactions, becoming even stronger. Unlike humans, with countless “copies” learning in parallel AI can explore and learn non-stop  with far more efficient than any human.

3. Entering Other Disciplines
Another major development in the direction of  AI research is its integration with other academic fields. It has become a driving force for their progress. In recent years, almost every discipline has begun using AI tools, with breakthroughs in all of them.

A famous example is DeepMind’s AlphaFold, which revolutionized biochemistry. AlphaFold predicts protein structures, and allows a process that once required years or even decades of lab experiments  to now be done in minutes. This breakthrough has profoundly influenced molecular biology, medicine, physiology, and pharmacology. In 2024, the Nobel Prize in Chemistry was awarded to AlphaFold’s creators—a well-deserved honor. Similar transformations are happening in mathematics, astronomy, physics, engineering, and more. These changes may have even greater impact than ChatGPT or Sora.

4. Peaceful Coexistence with AI
AI’s rapid progress forces us to re-examine its possible societal impact,. For example, ChatGPT and other large models sometimes generate incorrect or misleading information, which can spread and make information filtering harder. In addition, AI-powered drones, self-propelled artillery, and other autonomous weapons are being used in warfare, which could threaten human safety. In the future, if AI surpasses human abilities in all areas, preventing potential threats to society will become an urgent issue. Regardless, AI’s development is now an unstoppable reality. Learning to coexist with AI—making use of its strengths while avoiding its dangers—is an essential skill for every modern citizen.

Food for Thought
The integration of AI with basic sciences is inevitable because these sciences were originally built through the wisdom of earlier human scientists. Once AI’s “intelligence” reaches a certain level, it may expand the boundaries of a discipline just like human scientists—or even create entirely new ones. Imagine if machines could one day discover new natural laws on their own, what role should human scientists play then?
